Academic Rigor & Peer Review

End-to-End Manuscript Review Lifecycle

6-stage double-blind review workflow to ensure premier research standards
Slide 07 / 10
1
Portal Submission
Corresponding author uploads manuscript in IEEE/Springer format with 250–300 word abstract.
2
Desk Triage
Secretariat screens formatting and similarity check (Turnitin/iThenticate < 15% threshold).
3
Double-Blind Review
Assigned to 2 independent domain reviewers with anonymized author details across tracks.
4
Editorial Decision
Convenor Dr. Atif Hussain & Track Chairs review scores: Accept, Minor/Major Revision, or Reject.
5
Camera-Ready
Accepted authors incorporate reviewer comments and submit verified camera-ready copy by 20 June.
6
Oral Presentation
Author registers and presents physical oral or poster paper in designated technical hall on 23-24 July.
Evaluation Criteria
• Originality & novelty of contribution
• Technical soundness and experimental validation
• Clarity of presentation and relevance to track theme

Technical Strategy & Infrastructure

Submission Architecture: Option A vs. Option B

Evaluation of Microsoft CMT vs. Custom In-House Cloud Stack for NSIT Secretariat
Slide 08 / 10
Recommended Strategy
Option A: Microsoft CMT
Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit — Industry standard used by top ACM/IEEE conferences.
  • ✓ Zero Cloud Cost: Hosted and maintained entirely by Microsoft Research free of charge.
  • ✓ Built-in Double-Blind Tools: Automated conflict of interest detection and reviewer bidding.
  • ✓ Reviewer Familiarity: Reviewers from IIT Patna and NIT Surathkal already possess active CMT accounts.
  • ✓ Seamless Integration: Single button on our NSIT website directs authors straight into the ETIMS 2027 CMT track.
Ready in 48 hours with zero server overhead.
Option B: In-House Cloud Stack
Custom submission portal built on PostgreSQL/Supabase & NSIT campus servers.
  • • Total Customization: Fully white-labeled UI matching NSIT campus colors perfectly.
  • • On-Premises Data: Complete ownership of author files on college storage.
  • • Maintenance Overhead: Requires server patching, SMTP email deliverability, and database backups.
  • • Development Timeline: Requires 3-4 weeks of full-stack testing and security audits.
Alternative if institutional data residency is required.
