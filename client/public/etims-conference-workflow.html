Academic Rigor & Review Mechanics

The 6-Stage Manuscript Lifecycle

Detailed step-by-step breakdown of how a research paper moves from initial intake to final presentation and proceedings indexing.

01
Jan – 30 Mar 2027

Manuscript Intake Portal

Author registers on the portal and submits their original paper in standard double-column IEEE/Springer format. Author names are stripped from the main manuscript file.

Required Metadata
Paper Title, Abstract (250–300 words), 5 Keywords, Target Track (T1–T8), All Co-Authors' Affiliations.
Automated System Action
Generates unique Tracking Code (e.g., ETIMS27-P142), sends automated email acknowledgement, and stages file in secure storage.
02
Within 5 Days of Submission

Desk Triage & Plagiarism

The Convenor Secretariat screens incoming manuscripts for basic quality, scope alignment, and originality before sending to reviewers.

Plagiarism Threshold
Similarity index must be strictly < 15% overall (excluding references) and < 3% from any single source using Turnitin/iThenticate.
Outcome Branches
Pass -> Advanced to Track Chair. Fail -> Desk Rejection or 7-day formatting revision request sent to author.
03
1 Apr – 10 May 2027

Double-Blind Peer Review

Two independent reviewers are assigned per paper. Reviewers cannot see author identities or institutions, eliminating prestige bias.

Review Panel Pool
Drawn from IIT Patna, NIT Surathkal, GEC Munger, GEC Bhojpur, and senior NSIT faculty across 7 departments.
Automated Safeguard
Conflict of Interest (COI) detector prevents reviewers from evaluating papers from their own institution or department.
04
By 31 May 2027

Convenor Decision Desk

Conference Convenor Dr. Atif Hussain and Track Chairs review reviewer scores and synthesize feedback into a definitive decision.

Evaluation Rubric (100 pts)
Novelty (20%), Soundness & Math (30%), Experimental Validation (20%), Structure & English (15%), Track Fit (15%).
Decision Categories
Accept (Ready for camera-ready) | Minor Revision (7-day turnaround) | Reject (Constructive feedback).
05
By 20 June 2027

Camera-Ready & Copyright

Accepted authors revise their papers according to reviewer recommendations and upload final production-ready files.

Submission Requirements
Final PDF, editable source files (Word DOCX / LaTeX ZIP), and signed institutional Copyright Consent Form.
Prerequisite Rule
At least one registered author with confirmed fee payment is mandatory for inclusion in the official proceedings.
06
23–24 July 2027

Oral Presentation & Awards

Physical presentation in assigned conference hall at NSIT Bihta before domain session chairs and peer audience.

Presentation Formats
Oral Presentation: 12 min presentation + 3 min Q&A. Poster Session: Dedicated 60 min exhibition in central foyer.
Award Evaluation
Session chairs score presentations for Best Paper, Best Student Paper, and Best Poster Awards presented at valedictory.
Financial Reconciliation & Attendee Intake

Delegate Registration & Payment Verification Flow

How delegates register, pay via UPI / Gateway, get verified by NSIT accounts, and check in on-campus.

1

Category Selection

Delegate chooses one of 5 verified tiers: Faculty (₹2,500), Industry (₹3,500), PG/Scholar (₹1,500), UG Student (₹800), Attendee (₹500). System applies Early-Bird or Regular tariff automatically.

Input Fields
Full Name, Email, Phone, College/Org, Presentation Type (Oral / Poster / Observer).
2

Payment Settlement

Delegate pays via official NSIT Conference QR Code (SBI/PNB/Canara Bank) or online payment gateway. Enters 12-digit Bank UTR / Reference Number.

Payment Modes
UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM) or NEFT/RTGS Bank Transfer.
3

Accounts Reconciliation

NSIT Finance Desk checks bank ledger against submitted UTR number. For student categories, verifies valid college ID card. Clicks 'Approve'.

Turnaround Time
Instant (for Payment Gateway Webhooks) or within 24 hours (for Manual Bank UPI verification).
4

Pass & On-Campus Check-In

System generates verifiable digital Delegate Pass with unique Pass ID (e.g. ETIMS27-D409) and QR code. Downloadable as PDF/TXT.

At NSIT Reception
Volunteer scans QR code on phone -> Handover of official delegate kit, lunch coupons, and physical lanyard badge.

Convenor's Setup Blueprint

Exact Setup Checklist: Accounts, Systems & Committees

What Dr. Atif Hussain, the college IT administration, and NSIT accounts must set up to launch operations.

💻 Software, Portals & Cloud Setup

Technical infrastructure required to manage papers, emails, and web traffic.

  • 1
    Microsoft CMT Conference Registration (Option A)
    Go to cmt3.research.microsoft.com, submit a Conference Request form for 'ETIMS 2027'. Enter Dr. Atif Hussain as Chair. Create 8 tracks (T1 to T8). Cost: ₹0 (Free by Microsoft Research).
    Est. Setup Time: 24–48 hours
  • 2
    Institutional Subdomain DNS Linkage
    Request NSIT IT admin to map etims2027.nsit.ac.in via DNS CNAME to our production institutional server cluster. Gives authentic college domain credibility.
    Est. Setup Time: 1 hour
  • 3
    Official Secretariat Mailbox Verification
    Ensure etims2027@nsiterp.in has SPF, DKIM, and sufficient sending quota (Google Workspace / Outlook 365) to send batch acceptance letters without spam filtering.
    Est. Setup Time: 2 hours
  • 4
    Turnitin / iThenticate Screening Account
    Verify NSIT Central Library Turnitin institutional license is active for plagiarism checking during desk triage. Threshold locked at <15%.
    Est. Setup Time: Ready via Library

🏦 Banking, Payment & Campus Setup

Financial account, reception counters, and logistical execution.

  • 1
    Dedicated Bank Account / Merchant UPI QR
    Coordinate with NSIT Accounts office to generate a dedicated conference UPI QR Code with name 'NSIT ETIMS 2027'. Avoids mixing conference delegate fees with general college fees.
    Responsible: Finance Section
  • 2
    Reviewer Invitation Letters to Advisory Board
    Send formal invitation circulars signed by Convenor Dr. Atif Hussain to our 7 advisory professors at IIT Patna, NIT Surathkal, GEC Munger, and GEC Bhojpur confirming their track assignments.
    Responsible: Convenor Desk
  • 3
    Student Volunteer Wing Allocation (6 Wings)
    Appoint departmental student volunteers across 6 wings: (1) Registration Desk, (2) Hall Management & AV, (3) Guest Reception, (4) Publicity, (5) Photography & Docs, (6) IT Support.
    Responsible: 5 Co-Convenors
  • 4
    Publisher Agreement / Proceedings MoU
    Finalize formal agreement with academic publisher (e.g. CRC Press / Springer / Atlantis) to define camera-ready templates and indexing specifications.
    Responsible: Patron & Convenor
